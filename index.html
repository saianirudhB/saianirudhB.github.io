— About

I started as a Django developer before DevOps was even in my job title — which is probably why I care more about whether a deploy works than whether the YAML looks clever.

At TCS, I was the person 12 repositories and six engineers came to when a release needed to go out cleanly. Over a year I pushed our typical release from an hour of manual steps down to fifteen minutes, containerised eight applications nobody wanted to touch, and reviewed 200+ pull requests without letting broken builds hit main.

Now I’m building the AWS and Kubernetes side of that skill set on my own time: a CI/CD pipeline that ships a Django API to ECR and EC2 on every merge, Terraform modules that provision a VPC and IAM roles the way a team would actually review them, and a Helm chart that puts Prometheus and Grafana in front of an EKS cluster.
